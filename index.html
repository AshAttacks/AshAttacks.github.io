About Me

My name is Richy Xie, and I'm a junior at Northeastern University. I'm from Long Island, New York, and I'm studying to work in the investments field. This upcoming summer, I'm working in New York City at an investment bank. I'm super excited to get started and apply some of the knowledge I've learned in class, along with getting to chat with some of the smartest people in the financial industry.

I have always been interested in learning more about machine learning and how I could apply my own interests in investments to the field. As a kid, I used to make Discord bots and always wanted to learn how the underlying code actually worked (instead of only following YouTube videos). Since then, I've had the opportunity to use some of my data science skills during my co-op at Verisk Catastrophe and Risk Solutions. While I was there, I acted as the lead risk analyst on a variety of different deals. Some of the data science skills I used were database querying (SQL), building a small machine learning model, and reporting on findings.
